\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Polynomial commitment schemes (PCSs) are an important cryptographic primitive underlying zero-knowledge succinct non-interactive arguments of knowledge (zkSNARKs).
The prover time of zkSNARK protocols is largely determined by the prover time of the underlying PCS;
hence, the efficiency of the PCS directly impacts zkSNARK efficiency.
