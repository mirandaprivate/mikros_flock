\begin{tablenotes}
	\item[Note]
	Costs are based on a size-$N$ arithmetic circuit with $m$ multiplication gates, divided into $d$ layers in some methods.
	$\epsilon \ge 2$ is a positive constant integer.
	$\lambda$ is the security parameter.
	Only dominant terms are shown.
	$\Field$ and $\Group$ represent 
	%the number of 
	field elements and DLOG/pairing group elements.
	$\muls$, $\hashes$, $\exps$, and $\pairing$ denote field multiplications, hashes, scalar multiplications, and pairings.
	QAP, LC, ACS, and R1CS stand for the arithmetization (Arith.) methods of quadratic assignment problem, layered circuit, algebraic constraint satisfiability, and rank-1 constraint system.
	RO, AGM, DLOG, UOG, SXDH, and $q$-type indicate assumptions (Assump.) of random oracle, algebraic group model, discrete logarithm, unknown-order group, symmetric external Diffie-Hellman, and the $q$-type (bilinear) Diffie-Hellman/knowledge of exponent.
